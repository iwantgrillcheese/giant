What I'd actually learn for a FanDuel career

  1. Implied probability & odds conversion. Translate +150 / -200 / 40¢ into the same mental language.
  2. Vig, hold & de-vigging. Understand where operator margin lives and how price quality changes by market.
  3. Expected value. Separate a good outcome from a good decision.
  4. Variance & sample size. Know why short-term results are noisy and why segmentation can lie.
  5. Correlation & conditional probability. Critical for SGPs, cross-sell, and customer behavior.
  6. Calibration. If you say 60% repeatedly, does it actually happen ~60% of the time?
  7. Then optional: Bayes, Kelly sizing, regression. Useful, but not where I'd start.
